## Context and constraints

BrandRadar measures how brands show up in answers from large language models, and how those mentions compare with competitors. I contributed to the product experience from April 2025 through November 2025. I do not own BrandRadar. The public company site is [brandradar.ai](https://www.brandradar.ai/).

Internal screenshots, customer data, and product metrics stay off this page.

## What I worked on

- Interactive dashboards
- Data visualization for brand and competitor mentions
- A responsive frontend
- AI workflows that read model responses
- API integrations behind that experience

## My role

Product engineer on the interface and the workflows around it. The contribution is the product experience: dashboards, visualization, and the AI workflows a user moves through. It is not ownership of the company or the dataset.

## Key decisions

- Keep the published story to the experience I actually shipped: dashboards, visualization, and AI workflows.
- Leave internal architecture, customer names, and performance numbers unpublished.

## Outcome

The contribution shipped inside the production product during that window. This page does not publish traffic, revenue, or model-accuracy numbers.
