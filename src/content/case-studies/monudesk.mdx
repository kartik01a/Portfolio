## Context and constraints

MonuDesk is a client product, an order management SaaS. I contributed to it. I do not own it. Customer data, private dashboards, internal URLs, and revenue figures are not on this page. The diagram below is the slice I worked on, not a map of the client's full system.

## The problem

The product needed order-management workflows that could talk to accounting and payments, with authentication and data flows that hold up in production.

## What I contributed

- SaaS features across the interface and the backend
- Authentication
- Database workflows
- A QuickBooks integration
- Payment integrations
- Support and product workflow integrations

## My role

Full-stack contributor: frontend architecture and components, backend services, authentication, PostgreSQL workflows, and the third-party integrations above.

## Challenges

The hard part was the integrations. QuickBooks and the payment providers had to stay in step with the product's own order data. I am not publishing the client's internal sync design.

## Outcome

Those workflows shipped inside the production SaaS: authentication, database workflows, QuickBooks, and payments. I do not have public metrics for this engagement, so none are listed here.
